Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding Kerala business strategy engagements.

How is a business consultant different from a digital marketer?

A digital marketer typically executes specific channels (SEO, ads, social). A business consultant operates at the executive layer — deciding channel prioritization, sequencing capital, and aligning unit economics. Abin Vinod bridges both by designing GTM roadmaps grounded in real daily search and ads execution.

Do you work with businesses outside Kerala?

The primary focus is Kerala-based enterprises, though digital-first engagements (SEO/GTM strategy, remote advisory) extend to national and international brands serving or expanding into the Kerala market.

What does a digital transformation roadmap actually include?

Typically: a comprehensive audit of existing digital spend and web assets, a prioritized channel strategy, technical infrastructure blueprints (GA4 tracking, search schema, CRM flow), and a phased quarterly execution plan.
