Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Digital-First Business Consultant do?

A Digital-First Business Consultant bridges high-level business strategy with modern digital execution. Rather than focusing solely on channel marketing, a business consultant evaluates customer acquisition economics, Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy, unit economics, tech stack optimization, and operational scalability.

How is a business consultant different from a digital marketing specialist?

A digital marketer manages tactical ad campaigns or search optimization. A business consultant works directly with founders and CEOs on revenue architecture, offer positioning, margin expansion, channel unit economics, and holistic business model transformation.

What is included in a Growth Audit & GTM Roadmap?

A Growth Audit scrutinizes your sales funnel, customer acquisition cost (CAC), lifetime value (LTV), competitive moat, and conversion bottlenecks. The resulting GTM Roadmap outlines clear quarterly milestones, channel selection, unit-economic targets, and implementation priorities.
