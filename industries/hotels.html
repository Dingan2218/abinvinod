The Real Problem — OTA Commission, Not Just Visibility

Most hotel marketing pitches lead with "get more visibility." The number that actually matters more is this: independent hotels are ceding roughly 63% of bookings to OTAs industry-wide, at commission rates typically between 15–25% — sometimes higher once preferred-placement fees are added (Sources: PhocusWire, SiteMinder, Cloudbeds 2026 Hospitality Reports). On a room that costs a guest ₹15,000, that's ₹2,250–3,750 gone before a single operating cost is covered.

Direct bookings, by contrast, cost roughly 4–5% all-in once payment processing and booking-engine costs are included. Shifting even 5–10% of bookings from OTA to direct is a meaningful, measurable margin improvement — not just a branding exercise. That's the number this strategy is actually built around, and it's the frame every recommendation below traces back to.

Where Kerala Hospitality Specifically Fits

Kerala's hospitality sector spans distinct property types with unique search dynamics and traveler intent profiles.

1. Houseboats & Backwater Tourism

(Alappuzha, Kumarakom, Kottayam) — Heavy OTA and TripAdvisor dependency, strong opportunity in direct-booking SEO and Google Business Profile for "near me" and seasonal search.

2. Hill Station Resorts & Homestays

(Munnar/Idukki, Wayanad) — Seasonal demand swings, strong visual/social channel fit, high dependency on review velocity and direct itinerary searches.

3. Beach Resorts & Coastal Retreats

(Kovalam, Varkala) — Competitive metasearch environment, requiring Google Hotel Ads, price accuracy feeds, and schema-backed content to capture high-intent travelers.

4. Ayurveda & Wellness Resorts

International wellness-tourism search intent, distinct from domestic leisure search, often higher-value long-stay bookings justifying dedicated marketing investment per guest.

What Direct-Booking Digital Strategy Actually Involves

  • Google Business Profile & Google Hotel Ads: The two essential channels every property type needs regardless of size, capturing travelers actively shortlisting properties.
  • Direct-Booking Organic SEO: Optimizing the property's own domain to rank for destination + stay keywords, driving visitors past OTAs directly to your booking engine.
  • Review Velocity & Reputation Strategy: Systematic review collection workflows, as review volume and recency directly affect both Google local pack rankings and guest conversion.
  • Schema-Backed & AEO Content: Implementing Hotel, Resort, and FAQ JSON-LD schemas to ensure AI search engines and search bots comprehend room amenities, rates, and location signals.
  • Mobile Booking Flow Optimization: Ensuring seamless mobile loading and friction-free booking funnel UX, preventing guest drop-offs on mobile devices.
  • Metasearch Interception: Running targeted Google Hotel Free Booking Links and Google Hotel Ads to capture direct clicks at significantly lower acquisition cost than OTA commissions.

Frequently Asked Questions — Hospitality

How much can a Kerala hotel realistically save by growing direct bookings?

Every booking shifted from a ~15-25% commission OTA channel to a ~4-5% direct channel is a meaningful per-booking margin gain — the exact savings scale with your average room rate and booking volume, which is worth calculating specifically for your property rather than using an industry-wide estimate.

Should my hotel abandon OTAs entirely?

No — OTAs still provide reach independent properties can't fully replace. The realistic goal is a healthier mix: use OTA visibility to be discovered, then convert repeat and referral guests to direct booking channels over time.

Is this different for houseboats vs. hotels?

Yes — houseboats have more concentrated seasonal demand and heavier reliance on TripAdvisor-style review platforms; hotels and resorts have more consistent year-round demand and a wider metasearch/OTA mix to manage.
